How it works

From data to insight to action — a seamless flow.

01

Connect

Salesforce CRM captures every member, donation, event, and interaction. Custom fields track engagement, satisfaction, and giving capacity.

02

Understand

AI analyzes patterns across your community — who's engaged, who's at risk, who has capacity to give more, and what messages resonate.

03

Engage

Automated workflows send personalized, AI-crafted messages via Telegram and email. High-value outreach goes through human approval first.
